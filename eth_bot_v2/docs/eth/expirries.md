ETH Option Expiry Schedule (Indian Time, Plain Language)
Daily Options: Expire every day at 1:30 in the afternoon (right after lunch).

Weekly Options: Expire every Friday at 1:30 in the afternoon.

Monthly Options: Expire on the last Friday of each month at 1:30 in the afternoon.

Quarterly Options: Expire on the last Friday of March, June, September, and December at 1:30 in the afternoon
